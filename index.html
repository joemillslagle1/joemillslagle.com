Questions people ask

What can you get for $1 million in Sarasota right now?

It varies a lot by area, by whether you want a condo or a single-family home, and by water access. Tell me your must-haves and I will show you what is actually available this week, then we can decide where your budget works best.

Is Siesta Key a good place to buy a $1M+ home?

It can be, depending on how you want to live. Think about beach access, condo or house, flood zone and insurance, association rules, and any rental restrictions. I will walk you through each one before you fall in love with a property.

How do I buy a $1M+ home in Sarasota if I live out of state?

You can do most of it from home. We start with a call about what you want, I send you homes by text and video, and we tour by video or in person when you visit. I stay in close touch by call and text through the offer, inspections and closing.

Should I buy a condo or a single-family home on Siesta Key?

A condo usually means less upkeep and an association to understand. A single-family home usually means more space and more responsibility. For a condo, ask for the current budget, reserve information and any special assessments before you offer.

How much does flood insurance cost on a waterfront home in Sarasota?

It depends on the flood zone, the elevation and how the home was built, so I would not guess a number. Get a quote from an insurance agent before you write an offer. Lenders require flood coverage in many flood zones, so it is worth knowing early.

Do I need a wind mitigation inspection on a $1M+ Florida home?

Many buyers order one. It documents the wind-resistant features of the home, and your insurance agent can use the report when quoting coverage. I can point you to inspectors and help you read the results.

How do I price a luxury home in Sarasota?

We look at recent comparable sales, the condition of the home, and what makes it different, then agree on a plan before it goes on the market. I will talk through that with you before you list, not after.

What should I fix before listing a $1M+ home in Sarasota?

Start with a walkthrough. I look for things a buyer's inspection is likely to flag, how the home presents, and what is worth doing before photos. I will tell you what to leave alone too, so you do not spend money you do not need to.

How do I sell my Sarasota home if I am moving out of state?

We plan the timeline around your move. We prepare the home, handle showings and updates by call and text, and keep you informed through the offer and closing, so you do not need to be here for every step.

How do I choose a luxury real estate agent in Sarasota?

Ask about experience at your price point, how they communicate, and who will actually work with you. I led a real estate team for 7 years. I have $75M+ personally closed, 200+ transactions, and I am glad to talk through any of it before you decide.
